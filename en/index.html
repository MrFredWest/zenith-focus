01 Sessions

Choose. Set an intention. Begin.

Focus, Meditation or Nap: set the duration, add an intention if you like, and go. 25 minutes, 50 minutes, or your own.

02 In session

Time, without the rest.

A gold ring, the time left, two gestures: Pause or End. Your soundscapes stay within reach at the bottom of the screen.

03 Rituals

A breath for every moment.

Heart coherence, box breathing, 4‑7‑8: guided techniques you can also create and reorder. Heart coherence is free; the others come with Full Access.

04 Rhythm

Notice your rhythm.
Facts, never a score.

Your streak, sessions, focus minutes, daily goal and practice calendar all live in their own tab.

Apple Watch

A moment for you,
on your wrist.

Start a Focus or a breathing session from your watch, even without your iPhone nearby. When the session ends, it is saved to your iPhone.

  • An adjustable duration for focus and for breathing.
  • Standard or gentle vibrations to guide your breath.
  • Your iPhone rituals, without sound.

Your space stays yours
